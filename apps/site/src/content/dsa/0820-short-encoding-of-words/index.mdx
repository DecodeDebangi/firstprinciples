## The problem

All the words must be written into one string, each word followed by a `#`. A word is allowed to be read from inside another word's entry, starting partway and running to its `#`. Return the length of the shortest such string.

For `same, me, game, ame, toy` the answer is 14: the string `same#game#toy#` contains everything, since "ame" and "me" can be read from the end of "same#" (or "game#").

## The key question

> **Which words never need an entry of their own?**

Those that are the ending of some other word: they can be read from inside that word's entry, up to its #. Only the words that are nobody's ending need space.

## Approach 1: A set, removing every word's endings

Put the words in a set. For each word, remove each of its shorter endings from the set. What's left are the words that are nobody's ending; add up their lengths plus one # each.

<Viz client:visible approach="set" />

<Complexity time="O(Σ L²)" space="O(Σ L)">
**Time.** A word of length L has L − 1 shorter endings, and copying each out to look it up costs up to L letters: **O(L²)** per word.

**Space.** The set: **O(Σ L)**.

**What it wastes:** copying. Each ending is built letter by letter just to look it up.
</Complexity>

## Approach 2: A trie of the reversed words: count its leaves (optimal)

Insert every distinct word reversed. A word that is another's ending ends partway down that word's path, so only the leaves are nobody's ending. Add up each leaf's depth plus one.

<Viz client:visible approach="trie" />

<Complexity time="O(total letters)" space="O(total letters)">
**Time.** One step per letter to insert, and one pass over the trie: **O(total letters)**.

**Space.** The trie: **O(total letters)**.
</Complexity>

## Common mistakes

- **Counting a repeated word twice.** Use each distinct word once.
- **Looking at beginnings instead of endings.** "me" fits inside "game#" only because it's at the end, followed by #.
- **Counting every ✓ in the trie.** Only words at the leaves need space; the others are inside a longer word.
