## The problem

You get a list `height`, where `height[i]` is the height of a vertical line at position `i`. Any two lines form a
container with the x-axis. It holds `(j − i) × min(height[i], height[j])` units of water. Return the most water any
pair can hold.

For `height = [3, 7, 2, 6, 4, 8, 5]` the answer is `28`: lines 1 and 5 are 4 apart and the shorter is 7, so `4 × 7 = 28`.

## The key question

> **Once I've measured a container, which of its two lines can never be part of a bigger one?**

The shorter line. Every other container that uses it is narrower, and its water level can't rise above that
short line. So after measuring, you can drop the shorter line for good.

## Approach 1: Brute force, every pair

Measure every pair of lines and keep the largest.

<Viz client:visible approach="brute" />

<Complexity time="O(n²)" space="O(1)">
**Time.** `n(n − 1)/2` pairs: **O(n²)**.

**Space.** A couple of numbers: **O(1)**.

**What it wastes:** it keeps measuring containers built on a short line that has already been beaten.
</Complexity>

## Approach 2: Two pointers, move the shorter line (optimal)

Start with the widest container: `lo` at the left end, `hi` at the right. Measure it, then move whichever pointer
sits on the shorter line inward. Repeat until they meet.

<Viz client:visible approach="pointers" />

<Complexity time="O(n)" space="O(1)">
**Time.** Each step moves one pointer inward, so `n − 1` steps: **O(n)**.

**Space.** Two pointers and the best area: **O(1)**.

**Why it's safe:** dropping the shorter line only rules out containers that are narrower *and* no taller than the
one just measured, so none of them could be the answer.
</Complexity>

## Common mistakes

- **Moving the taller line.** That can only shrink the width without raising the water level. Always move the shorter one.
- **Using `max` for the height.** Water spills over the shorter line: the level is `min(height[lo], height[hi])`.
- **Confusing it with Trapping Rain Water.** Here only two lines matter, and the lines between them don't block anything.
