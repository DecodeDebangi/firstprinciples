A [trie](/concepts/tries/) is fast at one kind of question: which words **begin** with these letters? Some problems
ask about **endings** instead. The fix is to turn them around: write every word backwards before inserting it. A
word's ending is then the beginning of its reversed form, and the ordinary trie walk answers the question.

## Does a word end here?

Letters arrive one at a time, and after each one you must say whether some word ends exactly at the latest letter.
Read the letters so far **backwards**, newest first, through a trie of reversed words. Reaching a ✓ means a word ends
here; falling off the trie means none does. The walk never reads more letters than the longest word has.

<Viz client:visible approach="stream" />

That's [Stream of Characters](/dsa/stream-of-characters/).

## Which words are endings of others?

In a trie of reversed words, a word that is the ending of a longer word lies **on that word's path**: "me" reversed is
"em", which is how "game" reversed ("emag") begins. So the words that are nobody's ending are exactly the ones at the
**leaves**.

<Viz client:visible approach="encoding" />

That's [Short Encoding of Words](/dsa/short-encoding-of-words/), where only the leaf words need space of their own.

## A beginning and an ending at once

What about words that start with p **and** end with s? One trie can't follow both ends of a word. The trick is to
change what's stored: for each word, insert keys made of one of its endings, a separator, then the whole word, like
`e#ape`, `pe#ape`, `ape#ape` and `#ape`. A query for ending s and beginning p becomes the single prefix `s#p`. It costs
O(L²) keys' worth of letters per word, in exchange for O(L) queries. That's [Prefix and Suffix
Search](/dsa/prefix-and-suffix-search/).

## Interview theory

<QA q="How do you check suffixes quickly with a trie?">
Insert every word reversed. A suffix question about a string becomes a prefix question about the string reversed, which
is one walk down the trie.
</QA>

<QA q="How do you search by prefix and suffix together?">
Store, for every word, each suffix + "#" + the whole word in one trie, keeping on each node the best index that reaches
it. A query walks suffix + "#" + prefix. Building is O(L²) per word; each query is O(L).
</QA>
